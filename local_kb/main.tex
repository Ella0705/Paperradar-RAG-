\section{Introduction}
My current research interests 
- Main direction: Architectural evolution and feature representation research of foundation models. Incorporating multi-modal learning paradigms of deep residual networks (ResNet) and bidirectional Transformer (BERT).
- Current project: Developing a parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) framework based on LoRA technology for ultra-large-scale language models (such as GPT-3). The aim is to achieve rapid adaptation of SOTA models on consumer-grade GPUs.
- Areas of particular interest:
- Efficient model adaptation: Utilizing LoRA technology to achieve downstream task migration of large-scale models at extremely low computational costs.
- Exploration of emergence capabilities: Studying the performance and underlying logic of models like GPT-3 in Few-shot tasks.
- Generative adversarial paradigm: Exploring the application of GANs in synthetic data generation and enhancing model robustness. 
- Not very interested:
- Outdated NLP architecture: Limited interest in non-bidirectional representations or traditional language modeling methods that have been completely replaced by Transformers.
- Narrow domain supervised learning: Not inclined to study traditional supervised learning methods that lack a general pre-training foundation and are only targeted at a single task.
